# Code of conduct

Treat contributors, readers, victims, families, and source-agency staff with
respect. Discuss evidence and software behavior without harassment,
discrimination, threats, sexualized content, or personal attacks.

Do not publish private personal information, speculate about a person's guilt,
contact families through this project, or encourage interference with an
investigation. Correlation results are research leads, not evidence of a
shared offender.

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other project-managed spaces. Report concerns privately to
[courtney@latenttrace.org](mailto:courtney@latenttrace.org), sharing only the
information needed to understand the concern.

The maintainer may remove content, close a discussion, restrict participation,
or block an account according to the circumstances. Reports are handled
discreetly, but absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
